📸 Thumbnail Capture Tool

Automatically captures a frame from each of your 7 videos and lets you download them as thumbnails for the portfolio.
No software needed — runs entirely in your browser.

  1. Click "Capture All Thumbnails" — the tool will load each video and snapshot a frame.
  2. Wait for all 7 to complete (large files may take a moment to load).
  3. Click the Download button on each card, saving the file with the exact name shown (e.g. video-01.jpg).
  4. Move all downloaded files into assets/thumbnails/ inside your portfolio folder.
  5. Refresh index.html — thumbnails will appear on all cards.
Ready — click the button to start capturing.
